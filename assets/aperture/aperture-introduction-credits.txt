Aperture | A competitive intelligence product tour

Recorded in Aperture 2.1 using a dedicated project built from public multiple myeloma research. The consultant proposed the plan, saved the project and imported study records. The target comparison is an excerpt from a live Ask interaction. An analyst curated the FDA and publication benchmarks, registry milestones, and final brief for the tour. Waiting time is shortened. Enlarged details appear beside actual workbench captures. The closing slides come from the application's native editable PowerPoint export.
